# Planet disaster data: media credits

The case study uses the
[color wordmark from Planet documentation](https://docs.planet.com/img/logo.svg)
and embeds the
[Planet disaster-data catalog](https://source.coop/planet/disasterdata) in
the Portolan browser.

`public/img/case-studies/planet-general-santos.webp` is an urban crop of
scene `20260608_232426_ssc1_u0001`, extracted from its
[full-resolution RGB GeoTIFF](https://data.source.coop/planet/disasterdata/philippines-earthquake-2026-06-08/SkySatCollect/20260608_232426_ssc1_u0001_visual_file_format.tif).
The crop uses bounds 125.158°E, 6.1095°N to 125.182°E, 6.123°N, resampled to
2560 × 1440 with Lanczos, then cropped to pixel window 256, 240, 2048, 1152.
The RGB display range is 34–168, mapped to 0–255 with exponent 0.95.
