[Epoch](https://epoch.blue/about) provides first-mile supply-chain data to platforms, companies, and suppliers. Its work supports decisions about deforestation, forced labour, and carbon disclosure.

Epoch works to make the origins of value chains visible to the people making decisions about them. Its team brings experience in payments, climate technology, supply chains, and enterprise technology.
