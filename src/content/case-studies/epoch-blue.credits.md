# Epoch Blue: media credits

The case study uses the Epoch logo in color, and its light version on the
homepage card, both supplied by Epoch.

The case study list shows a screenshot of the
[Epoch open data explorer](https://jakenotjay.github.io/me/explorer/), by
Jake Wilkins, built with deck.gl-raster by Kyle Barron. The article embeds
that viewer directly.

The ghost forest video is by Epoch. It contains modified Copernicus
Sentinel data (2017–2025) and Nature Trace's Forest Typology v2 (beta). Its
final totals also use the Meta and WRI global canopy height map. The
supplier's outline is shown without any name or location label.
