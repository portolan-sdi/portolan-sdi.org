*Draft for Colton · Water data · Hydrology · Lincoln Institute*

[GeoConnex](https://docs.geoconnex.us/) connects water data across the United States. The [Lincoln Institute of Land Policy's Center for Geospatial Solutions](https://www.lincolninst.edu/centers-initiatives/center-geospatial-solutions/key-initiatives/) and the US Geological Survey maintain it as part of the Internet of Water Coalition.

Its [Portolan catalog](https://storage.googleapis.com/harvest-geoconnex-us/stac/catalog.json) publishes [GeoParquet](https://geoparquet.org/) and [PMTiles](https://docs.protomaps.com/pmtiles/) exports of the registry's features. On 2026-11-18, the root linked **38 collections**, including monitoring locations, watershed boundaries, and public water systems. The [collection metadata](https://storage.googleapis.com/harvest-geoconnex-us/stac/ref-hu12/collection.json) credits Colton Loftus at Lincoln's Center for Geospatial Solutions as the processor.
